## Adding photography or 3D models later

- **Photos**: place optimised files (AVIF/WebP, ≤ 2400 px wide) in `public/images/` and set `art.image` on the item
  (code or admin panel). Record the source, author and licence in the table below. Only use images you own or that are
  licensed for commercial use (e.g. commissioned, or Unsplash/Pexels licence — check each image).
- **3D models**: place Draco/Meshopt-compressed `.glb` files in `public/models/` and load with drei's `useGLTF`. Record
  the licence (CC-BY models require visible attribution).
